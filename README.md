# EduFamily AI

**AI-Enabled Family Decision-Support Platform for Engineering Education**

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://edufamily-ai.netlify.app/  
💻 **GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/Madan535/edufamily-ai

## About the Project

EduFamily AI is a responsive web-based prototype designed to help students and families make more informed decisions about engineering education.

It brings career-branch exploration, college discovery, and education budget planning together in one simple platform.

## Problem Statement

Choosing an engineering branch and college can be difficult for students and families. They need to consider student interests, available institutions, educational expenses, and other family needs. Relevant information may be spread across different websites, making comparison and planning challenging.

## Our Solution

EduFamily AI provides a single platform where students and families can explore engineering branches, discover colleges, and estimate education costs before making decisions.

## Key Features

- **Career Guide:** Suggests engineering branches using student interests and subject preferences, with transparent rule-based matching.
- **College Compare:** Provides a searchable directory of 75+ institutions across India, with state and district filters and links to official websites.
- **Budget Planner:** Estimates annual and full-course education costs and accounts for estimated family support or scholarships.
- **Family Budget Summary:** Generates a summary that users can copy and discuss with their family.
- **Responsive Design:** Supports desktop and mobile screens.
- **Simple Navigation:** Provides access to Home, Career Guide, College Compare, and Budget Planner.

## Technology Stack

- HTML5 — page structure
- CSS3 — styling and responsive layout
- Vanilla JavaScript — interactive features and calculations

## How to Use

1. Open the live website.
2. Explore the Career Guide and review the suggested engineering branches.
3. Open College Compare and search using available filters.
4. Use Budget Planner to estimate education expenses.
5. Review or copy the budget summary to discuss options with your family.

## Run Locally

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser.
3. No package installation or build step is required.

## Current Prototype Limitations

- Career suggestions use rule-based matching. The current version does not use a trained machine-learning model or call an external AI service.
- The college directory is a discovery aid, not a complete list of every engineering college in India.
- College courses, fees, admissions, hostel expenses, and placement information must be verified through official institutional websites and relevant admission portals.
- Budget results are estimates and may not include every expense, scholarship condition, or future cost change.
- Recommendations are for informational purposes and should not replace official information or qualified counselling.

## Future Enhancements
